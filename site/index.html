Part IV · The atlases

Two single-cell atlases of the embryo

They answer complementary questions. Calderon et al. ask when: a continuous, absolute-time view of chromatin and expression in every lineage. Wang et al. ask where: single-cell 3D spatial transcriptomes from embryo to pupa, integrated with scRNA and scATAC.

Science 2022

Absolute time from asynchrony

A neural network predicts each nucleus's collection-window centre hour from its profile, and does better than a lasso model (test MSE: RNA 2.54 vs 4.72; ATAC 5.26 vs 8.8). The result is a continuum of nuclear ages, validated against bulk RNA-seq and DNase-seq time courses.

Minutes-scale ZGA & Zelda

Zygotic genes switch on along predicted age in 5-min steps, while maternal and silent genes do not. Zelda-bound regions open before target expression, which supports a pioneer role.

A lineage map in time

The data give 268 RNA and 171 ATAC cell states (54 and 38 annotations) across nine 2-h windows, linked into acyclic directed graphs (edge weight >0.2). Diversity jumps between 6 and 10 h.

Comprehensive regulatory compendium

The accessible regions cover 85% of ~5,000 validated embryonic enhancers, 94% of 8,008 mesodermal CRMs and 96% of ~1 M ChIP-defined binding sites for 233 TFs. More than 40,000 new distal regions are found.

TF nomination

NNLS links RNA and ATAC clusters, and a motif–expression interaction model assigns TFs to germ layers and times (Sage → salivary gland, GATAe → midgut, Awh → epidermis). FigR builds GRNs at 10–12 h.

3D transcriptomes from embryo to pupa

Embryos are sampled every 0.5–2 h, plus L1–L3 larvae and pupae P12–P72. The data give 3D patterns for 338 genes with no previously reported in situ pattern. Integrating with scRNA-seq lowers background, as validated by FISH.

Metro map of differentiation

PhyloVelo velocity fields on co-embedded RNA+ATAC order the cell types of every tissue in the three germ layers, labelled with their top TFs: GATAe (Malpighian tubules), sage (salivary gland), Mef2, bin, srp, crp, and new candidates CG9727, CG12219, CG7368, CG12236, CG4360.

Where differentiation starts

Fat body differentiates in a decentralised way (scattered, mixed potency). Foregut/hindgut differentiate in a centralised way (clustered origins, ordered stages). CNS morphometrics link cell migration to lncRNAs CR30009/CR45388.

The midgut, cell type by cell type

The midgut splits into 23 subclusters (AMPs, EEs, ECs, copper cells, gastric caeca). R1–R5-like regions emerge at ~13 h in adult order, and stem cells are already regionally specified in larvae.

exex regulates copper cells

This homeodomain TF is expressed early in the copper-cell lineage and acts upstream of kay and lab, co-regulating 'dual-motif' targets. Knockdown and exexKK30 heterozygotes lose copper cells and copper homeostasis.

Methods primer

Concepts you need to read these papers

Combinatorial indexing (sci-)

Nuclei are split across plates and barcoded in rounds: tagmentation or RT, then ligation, then PCR. Each nucleus ends up with a unique barcode combination, which allows millions of nuclei without droplets. Coverage per nucleus is lower, but numbers are huge.

scATAC-seq & motif accessibility

Tn5 inserts into open chromatin. Peaks are putative enhancers and promoters. Aggregating peaks that contain a TF's PWM (chromVAR-style deviations) gives a 'motif activity' per cell. TF families share motifs, so paralogs have to be told apart with expression.

Inferred age vs pseudotime

Pseudotime is relative progress along a trajectory. Inferred age (Calderon) is trained against real collection times, so it is in hours and comparable across lineages. That makes it possible to say "gene X turns on in the mesoderm 30 min before Y".

Stereo-seq

A DNA-nanoball-patterned chip with spatial barcodes captures polyA RNA from a tissue section at sub-micron spot pitch. Nucleus staining plus segmentation groups spots into single-cell bins, and Spateo aligns serial sections into a 3D embryo.

Label transfer & integration

Seurat anchors or NovoSpaRc map annotations or expression from deep scRNA-seq onto sparse spatial bins. CCA co-embedding pairs ATAC and RNA cells (FigR) when they were not measured in the same cell.

GRN inference

SCENIC finds TF regulons as co-expressed targets with motif support. Pando and FigR use peak–gene links plus motifs, and NNLS decomposes one modality as a mixture of clusters from the other. All of these generate hypotheses to test with genetics, as with exex.
